## 07:44 PST · WL-2026-11-10-001 · 2.1: the menu bar popover is readable in light and dark (solid popover, cards and bars; stronger text)

**Type:** fix · **Status:** done · **Tags:** 2.1,colours,readability

### What
The founder: "I need you to change the background color of the app to make it easier to read things. Right now its next to impossible to read anything because of the terrible background color. It needs to be clearly readable in both light mode and dark mode on the mac." Built and merged as `53bde1c` (COLOURS) on `main`. Not pushed, not released (release: pending).

### Why
The popover was see-through twice over: `NSPopover` (system chrome, `StatusItemController.swift`) plus `MainMenuView`'s `.background(.ultraThinMaterial)`, with low-alpha card, tile and track fills on top, so the surface behind every word was his wallpaper. No contrast can be guaranteed against an unknown backdrop. The text tokens were weak even on a good one: system `secondaryLabelColor` is 3.95:1 on white, and the stock bar colors used as percentage text were 2.0:1 to 3.2:1 on their own tinted tiles; badges were white on coral (3.1:1) and on system green (2.0:1 to 2.2:1).

### How
- `PixelSwitch/Models/BrandColor.swift`: about ten adaptive tokens became opaque with computed values (light / dark): `popoverBackground` #F2F2F5 / #1C1C1E (new, replaces the material), `cardFill` #FFFFFF / #2C2C2E, `cardFillStrong`, `rowTile` (new, one solid tile per limit row), `cardBorder`, `tabFill`, `progressTrack`, `textSecondary` #55555C / #B4B4BA (fixes every call site at once), `subtleBrand`, `activeAccountRing` #E05A00 / #FF942E, and new `textOnFill` #111111 (text on the coral and green badges and the selected tab), `brandText`, `costGreen`, `limitOK` / `limitWarn` / `limitCrit`.
- Call sites in the popover, cards and Settings files: one solid `rowTile` instead of cardFill plus a 10% tint in `UsageLimitRow`; `Badge` text is `textOnFill`; `MenuBarConfig.stockColor(.dashboard)` uses the AA-safe `limit*` colors at the same 90 / 60 thresholds; inactive Accounts rows get `cardFill` instead of `.clear`; `.secondary` / `.tertiary` / `.quaternary` text became `textSecondary`.
- `Tools/screenshots/make_screenshots.swift` now paints `Color.popoverBackground` (not its own backdrop), uses the app's real `Badge`, and renders a Chrome sample (header, promo, tab bar, Accounts rows, cost row, footer) so those tokens can be sampled.

### Outcome
Measured on rendered PNGs (raw sRGB bytes, worst glyph pixel against its real background), light / dark: secondary text on a tile 6.62 / 5.50, on a card 7.39 / 6.75; percentage text critical 5.03 / 4.98, warning 5.19 / 5.52; Max badge 6.03 / 6.03; Active pill 8.51 / 9.34; bar fill against its track 3.44 to 3.93 / 7.47 to 8.42. Every body pair at least 4.5:1, every graphic at least 3:1, both appearances. Renders kept in `docs/investigations/2026-11-10-readability-ultra-auth/evidence/` (`limits-*`, `accounts-*`). The founder checks it by eye on card 1 of `docs/decisions/pixelswitch-hand-checks-2.1-2026-11-10.html`.

### Decisions
- Make the tokens opaque with computed hex values rather than switching to system materials or system colors: a fixed, measured surface is the only way to guarantee a contrast ratio. The cost is that the popover no longer picks up the wallpaper tint, which is the point.
- The Settings window and the sign-in window were left as system-native (opaque, grouped Form); only their text tokens changed.

### Lessons
- **Opaque surface tokens and text tokens must change together.** The text tokens (`textSecondary`, bar colors used as text) were tuned, loosely, against a translucent surface. Making fills opaque alone, or fixing text alone, leaves a pair nobody measured. Compute every pair (text on tile, text on card, bar on track, bar on tile) in both appearances.
- **The README screenshots sat on a solid backdrop, so the old readability fix never saw the translucency.** The screenshot harness painted its own opaque backdrop while the real `NSPopover` is see-through, so every earlier "it reads fine" check was made against a surface the app never has. The harness now paints the app's own `popoverBackground`.
- `NSBitmapImageRep.colorAt` shifts values (it reads #1C1C1E as #252528): sample `bitmapData` bytes instead.
- `ImageRenderer` cannot draw the real popover chrome (arrow, corner radius, edge), so the real popover is a hand check, not a render.

### Follow-ups
- `borderedProminent` button labels (white on coral, 3.1:1) were not touched; user-chosen custom limit-bar colors stay as chosen.
- `assets/screenshots` (the README's pictures) were not regenerated from the new harness.
- `UsageChartView.swift` is not referenced anywhere (dead code, found by the recon, left alone).
- `Tools/screenshots/make_settings_renders.sh` was already broken before this work (an `AssetColorStubs` clash).
- Widget (`PixelSwitchWidget/`) has its own `brandColor` and tints and was not part of this.

## 07:45 PST · WL-2026-11-10-002 · 2.1: never switch to an account that needs signing in, and leave a live login that dies (30-second watch); two reviews

**Type:** feature · **Status:** partial · **Tags:** 2.1,auth-expiry,switch

### What
Two requests, verbatim. "Also, something that happens is when switching sometimes authentication has expired and the Claude sessions then get stuck. The PixelSwitch app should check for this and not switch to an account that needs authentication." And: "Furthremore it should also check throughout the time an account is being used and if it detects that authentication is required again it automatically switches accounts. Again the idea is so agentic coding does not get blocked."

Built and merged: `f519f40` and `b345bfa` (AUTH-EXPIRY, merged `8cf6e27`), `6ee54f6` (AUTH-FIXES, merged `81b021c`). The last lane, `lane/auth-fixes-2` (rotation safety: `3ff3ce2`, merged `fb7d296` while this entry was being written), is in 2.1; this entry does not claim its test numbers. Not pushed, not released (release: pending). Status is partial because the founder has not seen it and the release is pending.

### Why
The real case, from `~/Library/Logs/PixelSwitch-app.log` on the founder's Mac, 2026-11-10: at 10:40Z Claude Code blanked the active account's login (the credential was 202 characters shorter, `claude auth status` exited 1). The 2.0 app never flagged a dead LIVE login (it logged "No token for <account>, skipping" every 5 minutes and returned nothing), so auto-switch sat still until the founder switched by hand at 13:39Z, three hours later.

### How
New `PixelSwitch/Services/AuthHealth.swift` (pure, in the unit harness): `AuthHealth` (the shape and the verdict), `LiveLoginWatch` (two-read confirmation), `DeadLoginPolicy` (what to do), `TargetRenewalPolicy` (when to renew a target).
- **Leave a dead live login.** A live login counts as dead only when Claude Code has BLANKED it (`claudeAiOauth` present, `accessToken` and `refreshToken` both empty, or `expiresAt` 0) AND `claude auth status` exits non-zero, confirmed on two reads at least 15 seconds apart. Never counted: an expired `expiresAt` alone, a usage 401, 429, 5xx, no network, a CLI timeout or launch failure, an unreadable credential, a credential with no `claudeAiOauth`, one failed read. AppState runs its own 30-second timer (`startAuthWatch`); each tick is one local Keychain read off the main actor with no log line, and the CLI is spawned only once the blank shape is seen (re-asked every 5 minutes while dead).
- **Reaction.** The account is flagged `needsReauth` (the existing flag and card: "Session expired. Re-authenticate (↻) to fix.") and the UNCHANGED engine is asked to switch through `isUnusable`, which answers "no subscription OR dead live login" and bypasses the 5-minute cooldown, exactly like 1.6's `noSubscription`. The target is never a Manual only, `needsReauth`, no-subscription or unswitchable account. Auto-switch OFF only flags, logs once and shows. A Manual only active account with a dead login IS left (`b345bfa`; see Decisions). No healthy target: stays put and says so once per change.
- **Never switch TO such an account.** The target's saved login is renewed first (`6ee54f6`: before EVERY switch, skipped only if this app renewed it within 300 s with the same token fingerprint). A server refusal flags the account and auto-switch tries the next target; no answer (offline, 5xx, 429, timeout) switches as before. A spent pair is never written live.
- After a switch off a dead login, the account just left has its saved login renewed and the server's verdict decides: a refusal keeps it flagged, a renewal that works clears the flag.

### Outcome
**First review (deciding, Opus, of `8cf6e27`): SHIP, P0 0, P1 0, P2 4, P3 9.** Settled from Claude Code 2.1.321's own bundle, read with `strings`, that its dead-token clear blanks a login only when the STORED refresh token is the one the server just refused, and that it re-reads the store and takes a different access token first ("race recovered"), so the watch cannot race Claude Code's refresh. Replaying the 10:40Z case: first sighting on the next watch read, confirmed 30 s later, then flag, evaluate, one fresh read of the target, switch: about 65 to 75 seconds in total. P2 findings: F1 the sign-in paths did not reset the watch (a just re-signed account could be switched away at once); F2 a failed re-read after a good renewal wrote the SPENT pair live; F3 the 60-minute renewal window let a revoked grant with a still-valid access token through; F4 the AppState wiring and the false-positive guard were pinned only by source text.
**Fix lane `6ee54f6` and the re-review (of `81b021c`): SHIP, P0 0, P1 0.** F1 fixed (`liveLoginReplaced()` runs in every place the live login is replaced), F2 fixed (`savedLoginAfterRenewal`), F3 fixed as the CTO decided (renew before every switch), F4 partly fixed (the pure functions `reaction`, `mayEvaluateNow`, `probe(after:)`, `mustRenewBeforeSwitch`, the `step` table, `savedLoginAfterRenewal`, `afterSwitchAttempt` are tested for real; the AppState and `ClaudeService` call sites are still text pins, so six mutations stay green: an inverted `mustRenew` gate, `leavesActive`, `renewed:`, the settle condition, a commented-out reset, `authStatusFailure`). New P3s: N1 (settle now runs as its own Task, so a quick re-sign can race its late answer), N2 (a crash between a successful renewal request and the save loses the only valid refresh token, hit on every switch now), N3 (no timeout on the renewal request, 60 s default). `lane/auth-fixes-2` (`3ff3ce2`) is the answer to N1, N2 and N3 plus a comment fix: it saves the rotated pair before the identity lookup, gives the renewal request a 15 second timeout, and drops a settle answer for a login that changed meanwhile.
Test counts: 1495 passed at `8cf6e27`; 1660 at `81b021c`; 1733 on `main` at `4294327` with the Ultra work merged in (conformance 150/150); 1763 on the release tree (`main` at `f71f451` minus the docs, after `3ff3ce2` and the last lanes merged; conformance 150/150).

### Decisions
- Engine and the 150 frozen conformance vectors untouched (the Linux port mirrors them; a change is a three-place change). The reaction goes in through `isUnusable`.
- Manual only + dead login IS left (matches 1.6 `noSubscription`; staying on a dead login blocks agentic coding). The first draft (`f519f40`) left it; `b345bfa` reversed that before merge.
- Auto-switch off never moves.
- Renew EVERY target before a switch (validates the refresh grant at the moment it matters; one request per switch, outside the usage and profile limiter). The 60-minute version was the P2 that F3 found.
- The `autoSwitched` event still reports `trigger: noSubscription` for a dead-login switch (the engine is frozen); only the LOG says `needsSignIn`.

### Lessons
- **Claude Code blanks a login only when the server refused its refresh token.** So a blank is strong evidence of a dead grant, and the saved copy of that login likely carries the same refused refresh token.
- The live-evidence note: on 2026-11-10 the founder switched by hand at 13:39Z back to account id prefix 318644E2 (ahamade@gmail.com, the active account when this was written), the one Claude Code blanked at 10:40Z. Its saved copy equalled the live login at 10:35Z, so it very likely carries the refused token. 2.0 has no watch and would sit on it again. 2.1's watch leaves it within about a minute if it dies again, and the account then shows "Session expired. Re-authenticate (↻) to fix."
- Any behaviour that lives in `AppState.swift` is only pinned by source text, because AppState is not in the unit harness: extract the decision into a pure function and test that, or the pin passes on an inverted implementation.
- A diagnosis experiment that reads a login must never send an expired token to Anthropic (it spends the quota and re-arms the one-hour block): the watch only reads locally.

### Follow-ups
- Not covered: Claude Code's `account_on_hold` refresh outcome and a past `refreshTokenExpiresAt` do not blank the login, so the watch cannot see them; whether `claude auth logout` blanks or deletes the item was not checked.
- The Usage card for a flagged account has no sign-in button (the Accounts tab refresh button and `pixelswitch accounts reauth <account>` work).
- Turning auto-switch ON after a confirmation made while it was off still waits for the next 5-minute refresh.
- Log lines still say "session" in places. The Linux port has no live-login watch.
- Hand check: none by hand, it cannot be caused on purpose. Card 3 of the 2.1 page says what 2.1 now does and what the log shows (`[authWatch]` lines, and `[autoSwitch] Rule needsSignIn`).

## 07:45 PST · WL-2026-11-10-003 · 2.1: Ultra (Max 20x) accounts have no 5-hour limit; the badge says Ultra; first-pass review and fixes

**Type:** feature · **Status:** done · **Tags:** 2.1,ultra,plan

### What
The founder: "Also, Anthropic got rid of the 5 hour limit on Max 200 accounts, now called Claude Ultra accounts. So that should be removed from those types of accounts." Built as `b0e9521` (ULTRA, merged `4b2e87b`) and fixed by `65c0e82` (ULTRA-FIXES, merged `4294327`). Mac only. Not pushed, not released (release: pending).

### Why
The usage API already sends `five_hour: null` for these accounts (2,463 reads on 7 accounts across `~/Library/Logs/PixelSwitch-app.log*`, 2026-11-03 to 2026-11-10, none with a numeric session), and the engine already treats a missing window as "cannot fire, not judged" (two conformance vectors pin it for Swift and Python). So the app only needed to stop drawing, tracking and describing a limit that does not exist, and to say Ultra instead of Max.

### How
- New pure `PixelSwitch/Models/AccountPlan.swift`: one definition of the plan. Ultra when the subscription is Max (or not known yet) and the tier is `max_20x` or `default_claude_max_20x`, or Claude Code's `planDisplayName` is "Ultra"; a tier that says 5x beats a display name that says Ultra; an unknown plan keeps the 5-hour limit. `Account.planTier` (nil never written) and `AuthStatus.planTier` (`claude auth status` already prints it). - One strip point, `AppState.requestUsage`: the engine, Usage card, menu bar, widget and control protocol never see a session reading for an Ultra account.
- Per surface: no Session row on the Usage card; the three session menu-bar modules are left out while an Ultra account is active (`MenuBarModule.visibleModules`, the saved list is not edited); the widget's four Session views are hidden (`WidgetAccountData.hasSessionLimit`, optional so old JSON decodes); Settings → Accounts shows a static "No 5-hour limit" (all five languages) in place of the Session menu; control protocol `AccountInfo.hasSessionLimit` (optional, revision 3 to 4, protocol version stays 2); CLI Session cell `n/a`, one "Switches at" number, `accounts threshold --session N` accepted and stored but the reply says it has no effect.

### Outcome
Review of `b0e9521` (first pass, Opus): SHIP-WITH-FIXES, 0 blocking, 3 P2, 4 P3; `bash Tests/run-unit-tests.sh` 1614 passed at `4b2e87b`. Fixed in `65c0e82` (lane tip 1687 passed; `main` at `4294327` 1733 passed, conformance 150/150):

- F2 and F3 (P2) the strip threw away a real `five_hour` reading, and a stale tier (a 20x account downgraded to 5x while not active) would hide a real session limit and let the engine switch onto an account whose 5-hour window is used up: `droppingEmptySession()` strips only a window with no utilization; `UsageAPIResponse.settingSession(for:)` plus the persisted `Account.sessionLimitObserved` make a real reading win until the API stops sending one.

F5 (P3) `PlanBackfillLedger` settles an account only after a read that returned data or a store with no backup for it; an unreadable store is retried, at most 3 times per launch. F6 (P3) the decisions that were only source-text pins are pure and tested with real inputs and inverted versions. F7 (P3) the stale "revision (3)" comment.

### Lessons
- The PreToolUse disk guard refuses any `hdiutil` whose device or mountpoint comes from a shell variable or substitution (it cannot prove the target is not the founder's "My Book" disk). `verify-release.sh` itself still runs (the hook reads only the command text). To inspect a DMG by hand, mount it with `hdiutil attach` using literal paths into the scratchpad and detach with the literal device (`hdiutil detach /dev/diskNN`).
- A `main` push alongside the tag push is normal and harmless (same commit, nothing publishes from it); only two TAG runs can race. `gh run watch` in zsh with no `timeout` binary: use `--interval 60 --exit-status`, and an until-loop Monitor for job-level progress.
- Test counts in the docs must name the tree they were measured on; the old 1733 was a real number on an older tree and had to be superseded, not just overwritten silently.
