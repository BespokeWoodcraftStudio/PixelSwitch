## PixelSwitch 1.4

### Changed

- **Update automatically is now on by default.** A new install of PixelSwitch keeps itself up to date without asking: it checks every 6 hours, downloads new versions in the background, and restarts into them when no account switch or sign-in is running. If you already chose, your choice stays as it is. To be asked before each update instead, untick **Settings → About → Update automatically**.

Your accounts, settings and saved logins carry over as they are.
